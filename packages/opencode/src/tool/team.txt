Coordinate a flat multi-agent team (lead + named teammates). Prefer 2 specialists. One action per call.

Actions:
- create: start a team; caller becomes lead. Args: name? (auto "team" if omitted), delegate?
- spawn: lead only. Start a background teammate. Args: member? (defaults to agent name), agent, prompt. name= and text= also work. Aliases: scout→explore, builder→build, research→researcher, docs→writer. Office/explore default worktree=false; build gets a worktree.
- message: send text. Args: to (member, lead, or *), text. member= and prompt= also work. Case-insensitive; @name ok.
- tasks: board. Args: task_action=list|add|claim|complete, title?, task_id?. Omitting task_action: title→add, task_id→claim or complete. Title can stand in for task_id.
- status: snapshot + a "next" hint. Call this when woken.
- approve / reject: lead only. Args: member
- merge: lead only. Merge a member worktree. Args: member
- shutdown: lead only. Stop one member or all. Args: member?
- cleanup: lead only. Disband team and remove worktrees

Rules:
- Members may only message / tasks / status
- Nested task subagents cannot use this tool
- Lead is auto-woken when members finish or message; do not end a turn only waiting
- If a call fails, read the error (it lists valid names/tasks) and retry
